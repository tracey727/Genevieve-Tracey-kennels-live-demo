Weather & Hazard Safety

LIVE WEATHER NOT CONNECTED

Run a clearly labelled fictional hazard exercise

Select a scenario to rehearse manager checks. This uses the Nature Has No Borders hazard categories, source/expiry checks and separate internal signal rules. It does not generate or obtain an actual emergency warning.

Weather exercise, welfare checks & acknowledgements

SIMULATED ONLY

Official information is separate from GENEVIEVE’s internal flags

Use Australia’s Bureau of Meteorology and the relevant state emergency authorities for current warnings. In a life-threatening emergency call 000. An internal AMBER/RED training status is NOT a BOM warning or authority to evacuate.

Real integration requires an authorised warning source, source/expiry/geometry verification, secure read-only Nature Has No Borders bridge, staff delivery confirmation, and independently tested fail-closed operation. Never claim all-clear on connection failure.
